What is Lyra?

Live, word-by-word lyrics that live on your Windows desktop.

Lyra is a lightweight system-tray app that reads whatever you're playing and lights its lyrics over your screen — one word at a time, in sync with the singer, with an ambient glow that reacts to the actual beat. It just lives there while you work, chill or vibe.

How the sync works

  • Reads the current track from Windows' media system — Spotify and YouTube Music, app or browser, no login.
  • Pulls free time-coded lyrics and lights words to real timing; smooths line-only timing when needed.
  • Listens to system audio (never your mic) to pulse the glow on every beat.

Make it yours

  • Themes: Ember, Neon, Aurora — one key swaps the whole look.
  • Thick bubble type, sized big, with a different entrance on every word.
  • Float it, resize it, or go full-screen. Album art shows beside the track.

Controls

  • Space play/pause · Shift theme · ← → skip · ↑ ↓ volume · F full screen · A glow.

Privacy

  • No microphone, no screen recording, no uploads. Lyrics fetched anonymously; everything else stays on your PC.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Free, v1.0.0.
